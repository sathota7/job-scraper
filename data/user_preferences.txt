Score higher for roles explicitly targeting candidates with fewer than 3 years of experience — titles like associate, coordinator, junior, or project manager I are the target sweet spot. These should score 6–9 depending on fit.

Do not penalize for industry. Project management skills transfer across all sectors — tech, healthcare, finance, creative, operations, and others are all equally valid. Only score down on industry if the role requires deep domain expertise (e.g., clinical trial PM requiring a medical background).

Score lower when job postings explicitly require 5+ years of experience; this is a hard disqualifier and no role demanding that threshold should score above 3.

Do not score above 4 for contract, temp, freelance, seasonal, or internship roles under any circumstances.

Penalize roles in news media significantly. If a position is primarily tied to news operations, broadcast journalism, or news-focused products, reduce the score by at least 2–3 points.

The user is an early-career professional targeting their first or second PM role. Calibrate seniority expectations accordingly — roles requiring extensive leadership or team management experience should be scored down even when the title sounds junior.
